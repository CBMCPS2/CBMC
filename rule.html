01 / FIELD CODE

Good teammates.
Better operations.

Professional courtesy, clear coordination and fair play. This is how we fight together.

  1. 01

    Respect the player.

    Treat one another with professional courtesy. Keep an open mind: different opinions are part of a healthy community.

  2. 02

    Ask the question.

    If you are unsure, ask. Questions help the whole squad learn.

  3. 03

    Follow the lead.

    Follow Ipdan and your squad leader (SL) or platoon leader (PL). Coordinate as one unit instead of splitting the objective.

  4. 04

    Keep a spawn alive.

    Use squad beacons thoughtfully. Check for an existing useful beacon before replacing it.

  5. 05

    Move with the squad.

    Use redeploy when the team needs to reposition. Listen to the call and regroup at the next objective.

  6. 06

    Fight for the faction.

    Play for the alert, help Vanu allies who ask, and never arrange planned double-teaming.
