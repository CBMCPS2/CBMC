HQ/Beacon field guide

02 / SQUAD BASICS

Your squad’s
way back in.

A beacon turns a long run into a coordinated drop. Use it to reach rooftops, reinforce a hot point or give your squad another angle.

BEFORE YOU PLACE

Check the colour.
Protect the spawn.

Each squad in a platoon has its own colour. Check the minimap for a useful beacon in your squad’s colour before placing another.

Only one beacon per squad can be active. Do not replace a good one without coordinating.

Archive note: the original guide lists a 300-second cooldown. Check the current in-game cooldown rather than treating this as a current patch value.

PLACEMENT CHECKLIST

Five steps to a useful drop.

01

Equip the beacon.

Join a squad, then select the spawn beacon. The original guide uses equipment slot 5; check your current key bindings.

02

Find a safe, open spot.

Look for a protected position with open sky above it. Give arriving squadmates a useful route into the fight.

03

Place it.

Left-click to deploy. Coordinate with the squad so you do not replace a better spawn.

04

Protect it.

If you have a Hardlight Canopy, use it to help protect the beacon.

05

Make the call.

Tell your squad where the beacon is. Then get back into the fight and support the incoming drop.
