BEFORE YOUR FIRST DROP

A few useful answers.

What should I bring to the squad?

A willingness to learn, ask questions and follow the objective. Infantry, pilots, drivers and builders all contribute to combined arms.

Where should I start with the guides?

Start with squad beacons and redeploy practice, then explore your role. The loadouts are historical references; check equipment in the current game.

When do you play, and do I need a microphone?

Ask the outfit about current sessions and communication expectations before joining a squad.

Does Discord membership join the in-game outfit?

No. Use the in-game outfit listing to apply. Discord is a place to meet the community and ask about playing together.
